Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Current website functionality: This website currently has no website contact form, user account, online checkout or payment facility, analytics platform, advertising pixel, embedded social feed, map, video player or intentional non-essential cookie/tracking system. Contact is offered through email and WhatsApp links.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy explains how RP Bookkeeping Consultant (Pty) Ltd (“RP Bookkeeping”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information in connection with this website and enquiries made through the contact methods published on it. It is written for a South African business and is intended to support compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

2. Information you may choose to provide

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, you may choose to provide information such as your name, email address, telephone number, business details, bookkeeping questions and other information contained in your message. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through an ordinary website enquiry.

3. Why we use personal information

Information received through email or WhatsApp may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss bookkeeping services, understand a prospective client’s needs, communicate about an existing relationship where applicable, keep appropriate business records and meet legal or regulatory obligations where applicable. We aim to collect and use information only for defined and reasonable purposes.

4. Website forms, analytics and tracking

There is currently no website form that stores submissions. The current site source does not intentionally include Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising trackers, behavioural tracking, local-storage based tracking or embedded social/media widgets. The site also does not intentionally use non-essential cookies.

Technical hosting or security infrastructure may create server logs or other strictly necessary technical records outside the website application itself. The exact hosting log fields, retention period and provider processing arrangements have not been independently verified here and must be confirmed by the business owner/hosting provider.

5. Email and WhatsApp

Email links open your chosen email application. WhatsApp links take you to WhatsApp or its services. Once you leave this website and use those services, the relevant provider’s own privacy terms and processing practices apply. RP Bookkeeping does not control how those third parties process information.

6. Third parties and international transfers

The website intentionally keeps third-party website dependencies to a minimum. No analytics, advertising or embedded third-party content is currently used. WhatsApp and email providers are external communication services. Any cross-border processing by a service provider, including hosting or communications providers, should be reviewed by the owner against POPIA section 72 and the provider’s current contractual/privacy terms before the business relies on this policy as a complete processing inventory.

7. Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, a lawful business requirement or another applicable legal obligation. This website does not publish a fixed retention schedule because the actual retention periods for business records, email, WhatsApp conversations and hosting logs require confirmation from the business owner and relevant service providers.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be maintained to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, disclosure or alteration. The website uses HTTPS when served through its configured secure domain and this version includes a restrictive content security policy and avoids unnecessary tracking dependencies. This page does not claim that any particular encryption, backup, access-control, breach-response or internal information-security process exists unless it has been separately verified.

9. Your rights

Subject to POPIA and other applicable law, individuals may have rights concerning access to, correction of and objection to certain processing of their personal information, as well as other rights provided by law. Requests can be sent to rikapowell80@gmail.co.za. We may need to verify identity and may lawfully decline or limit a request where an applicable exception or legal obligation applies.

10. Privacy contact and Information Officer

For website privacy enquiries, use rikapowell80@gmail.co.za. Owner action required: the business should confirm the formally designated Information Officer (and any Deputy Information Officer, if applicable), publish the correct details, and maintain its required POPIA/PAIA documentation and procedures.

11. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how your personal information has been handled, you may use the applicable complaint channels of the Information Regulator of South Africa. The Regulator provides POPIA complaints and compliance resources through its official services.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website, communication channels, processing activities or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date will be changed when material updates are made.
