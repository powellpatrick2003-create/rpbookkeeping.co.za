Cookie Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Current status: No application-controlled non-essential cookies, analytics or advertising trackers are intentionally used by the current website.

1. What the current site uses

The current website source does not intentionally set analytics, advertising, social-media or behavioural-tracking cookies and does not intentionally use local storage for tracking. There is no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising network, embedded social feed, map or video player in the current site.

2. Necessary technical processing

The website is hosted on web infrastructure that may create ordinary technical server records needed to deliver and secure the site. Those records are not controlled by the page JavaScript. The exact cookies, logs, retention periods and infrastructure processing used by the hosting environment should be confirmed by the owner/hosting provider.

3. WhatsApp and email links

WhatsApp and email are provided as ordinary links rather than embedded widgets. Following those links may take you to a third-party service with its own technologies, cookies and privacy practices.

4. Cookie consent

Because the current website does not intentionally deploy non-essential tracking technologies, a consent banner has not been added merely for appearance. If analytics, advertising, embedded third-party content or other non-essential tracking is introduced later, the cookie inventory and consent approach should be reviewed before deployment.

5. Owner action

Owner action required: confirm the hosting provider’s technical cookie/log behaviour and confirm that no other third-party scripts, plugins, DNS services or marketing tools are installed outside this website source.

6. Changes

This policy will be updated if the website’s technology or tracking practices change.
