COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions deserve clear answers.

A few things to know before starting a conversation.

What businesses do you work with?

RP Bookkeeping works with independent professionals, entrepreneurs and growing small businesses that want accurate, organised books and practical financial support.

Do you offer monthly bookkeeping?

Yes. Monthly bookkeeping can include transaction categorisation, reconciliations and regular reporting, based on the needs and systems of your business.

Can you help if my books are behind?

Yes. Catch-up and clean-up work is intended to bring overdue or untidy records back into order before establishing a sustainable routine.

Can you work remotely?

Yes. RP Bookkeeping can support clients remotely, with communication and document workflows set up to suit the business.

What happens in the first conversation?

We discuss your business, current bookkeeping position, priorities and whether the service is a good fit. Any scope, fees and engagement terms are confirmed separately before services begin.

How do I get started?

Use the WhatsApp or email details below. Tell me a little about your business and what you need help with, and we can take the next step from there.
