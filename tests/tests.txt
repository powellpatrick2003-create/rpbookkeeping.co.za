# Tests

## Test 1 - Primary booking journey [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: hero CTA, direct WhatsApp conversation, pre-filled enquiry message, core conversion path
Description: Verifies the main call-to-action gives a visitor a clear route to start a conversation.
Steps:
1. Open the homepage
2. Select "Book a conversation"
3. Follow the WhatsApp destination
Expected: The booking CTA is visible and opens a direct WhatsApp conversation with the configured RP Bookkeeping number and pre-filled enquiry message in a new tab.

## Test 2 - Mobile conversion and navigation
Viewport: mobile
Covers: responsive navigation, sticky mobile booking CTA, About section, FAQ interaction, mobile readability
Description: Verifies the most important conversion path remains easy to reach while scrolling on a phone-sized viewport.
Steps:
1. Open the homepage at 375x667
2. Confirm "Book a conversation" remains available while scrolling
3. Use the navigation to open the About section and then the FAQ section
4. Expand a FAQ question
Expected: The mobile booking CTA is reachable without covering important content, About and FAQ sections are reachable, the answer expands, and there is no horizontal page overflow.

## Test 3 - About and service journey
Viewport: desktop
Covers: About section, audience positioning, service discovery, process explanation
Description: Verifies a visitor can understand who RP is for, what RP provides and what happens before services begin.
Steps:
1. Open the homepage
2. Review the About RP Bookkeeping section
3. Continue through Services and How It Works
Expected: The page clearly explains the business audience, service categories, and the conversation/agreement/process flow without unsupported testimonials, certifications or guarantees.

## Test 4 - Accessibility and keyboard focus
Viewport: desktop
Covers: skip link, keyboard focus, semantic headings, details/summary accessibility
Description: Verifies core keyboard and semantic accessibility features remain present.
Steps:
1. Load the homepage
2. Press Tab until the "Skip to content" link receives focus
3. Continue tabbing through navigation and FAQ controls
Expected: Focus is visibly indicated, the skip link is usable, and FAQ controls are keyboard accessible.

## Test 5 - Legal, trust and SEO foundations
Viewport: desktop
Covers: legal links, privacy/terms/cookies pages, canonical metadata, structured data, truthful trust messaging
Description: Verifies the site retains its compliance and search foundations while presenting only supported business claims.
Steps:
1. Open the homepage
2. Inspect the footer legal links and page metadata
3. Open Privacy, Terms and Cookies
Expected: All three legal pages are reachable, canonical metadata is present, structured data uses the business details already published by the site, and no fabricated reviews/certifications/statistics are displayed.
